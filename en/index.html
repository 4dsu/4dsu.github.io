Hi, I’m 4dsu.

I’m a telecommunications engineering student. I love understanding how things really work: from hardware and networks to efficient software and personal tools.

I’m especially interested in systems programming, predictable code and building tools that solve concrete problems without needless complexity.

This website is an island you can explore. This is the classic version: all the content, no game.

How to play

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys (Shift to run) and press E in front of a place to open it.

You can also click the ground to walk there, or click a building or a frame to open it.

Tap the ground to walk there and tap a place to open it.

Press C (or the Camera button) to take photos of the island. They stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere.

Suki listens to you: press G (or the Suki button) to give her commands. If you find her ball, throw it with B.

There’s a paraglider on top of the Pedraforca, a hot-air balloon in Igualada, a bike at the house (X to get on), a rowing boat in the Delta to row and fish, and the metro links the coast with the mountains.

The island has the time, the season and the weather it has right now in Catalonia. Sound is off: the Sound button turns it on.

P: Game Boy colours (once you have the cartridge). F3: how it’s made.
